Who We Are

A creative studio
built on passion.

LIKHAera is a small creative studio born from a deep love of illustration and personalized art. We believe every person has a story worth celebrating — and we specialize in turning those stories into beautiful, handcrafted keepsakes.

From custom chibi portraits to adorable pet illustrations, every piece we create is drawn with intention, printed with quality materials, and packaged with genuine care.

✦

Our Mission

To celebrate creativity and personal stories through beautifully handcrafted art products. We believe every person deserves something made just for them — a tiny piece of art that carries real meaning and brings lasting joy.

◎

Our Vision

To become the Philippines' most beloved creative brand for personalized art merchandise — a place where custom art is joyful, accessible, and deeply personal for everyone who treasures handmade things.

The Process

4 Simple Steps

01

Choose Your Product

Pick from Stickers, Bookmarks, or Keychains. Browse our collection for style references and inspiration.

02

Share Your Vision

Tell us your design ideas — reference photos, colors, and any details. The more you share, the better we can bring it to life.

03

Submit Your Request

Fill out the order form below. We'll review it and reach out within 1–2 business days to discuss and confirm.

04

We Bring It to Life

You'll receive a digital preview before production. Once approved, your piece is crafted and shipped with love.
